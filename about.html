About Us

We are a global community of technologists, thinkers, and builders working together to keep the Internet alive and accessible for everyone.

Our Mission

To ensure the Internet is a global public resource, open and accessible to all. We believe in an Internet that enriches the lives of individual people — and helps create a healthier, more connected world.

Our Guiding Principles

  • The Internet is an integral part of modern life
  • Access to the Internet should be universal and affordable
  • Open standards and innovation make the Internet stronger
  • Individuals' security and privacy on the Internet are fundamental
